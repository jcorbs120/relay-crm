Need a starting point? Download the template CSV.

How duplicates are handled

Contacts are matched on email address. Colleagues' addresses are skipped, and existing contacts keep their current owner. If the email already exists you can choose to update that record (fill blank fields and merge tags) or skip it. Organisations are matched by name, then by email domain, and created if missing.

Moving from ACT!?

In ACT! go to File → Export → Text-delimited or Excel, pick Contact records, and include the fields you want. The "Company", "ID/Status", "Categories" and "Business Phone" columns map automatically.
